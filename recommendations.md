# Growth Recommendations

## 1. Improve the Product-View-to-Cart Experience

Prioritize improvements to product pages to encourage users to move from product discovery to cart addition.

Potential experiments include:

* Stronger and more visible add-to-cart CTAs
* Clearer product value propositions
* Better product imagery
* Reviews and social proof
* Transparent pricing and shipping information
* Clear product availability

**Primary metric:** View → Cart conversion rate.

---

## 2. Improve Google Traffic Quality and Intent Alignment

Google is a major source of product-view traffic, but its View → Cart conversion is relatively low.

Potential actions include:

* Improving product-page SEO
* Targeting more specific, high-intent search terms
* Improving product titles and descriptions
* Aligning search-result messaging with landing-page content
* Improving the relevance of pages users reach from search

The objective should not simply be to increase Google traffic, but to attract **more qualified product traffic**.

---

## 3. Validate Changes Through Experimentation

The current analysis identifies patterns and opportunities but does not establish causality.

Product-page improvements should therefore be tested through controlled experiments such as A/B tests.

Success should be evaluated primarily through:

**View → Cart → Checkout → Purchase**

rather than traffic volume alone.

---

## 4. Investigate Retention After Conversion

Improving conversion addresses the acquisition-to-purchase funnel, but sustainable growth also depends on whether customers return.

The next stage of analysis will therefore examine **cohort and retention behavior** to determine:

* Whether customers return after their first purchase
* How retention changes across cohorts
* Where the largest retention drop-offs occur
* Whether acquisition improvements could translate into long-term customer value
