# PHASE 8 — Final Business Strategy 🎯

## 1. Finalize Strongest Insights

### Acquisition
- High-volume traffic sources generate many product views, but not all sources translate into strong downstream conversion.
- Google is a major acquisition source and therefore has a significant impact on overall funnel performance.
- High-volume sources with relatively low view-to-cart conversion may indicate a gap between traffic intent and product engagement.

### Activation / Engagement
- A substantial number of users who view products do not progress to adding items to the cart.
- The largest opportunity is earlier in the funnel, between product discovery and cart addition.
- Device-level funnel performance is relatively similar, so device type alone does not explain the major conversion differences.

### Conversion
- Users who reach checkout are substantially more likely to complete the purchase than users earlier in the funnel.
- This suggests that the product-view → cart stage is a more important area for investigation than the final checkout stage.
- Traffic-source differences indicate that acquisition quality and user intent may influence conversion.

### Retention
- Cohort analysis shows a sharp drop in returning users after the first month.
- Later-month retention is considerably lower than initial cohort activity.
- This indicates that repeat engagement is a major area for potential growth.

---

## 2. Final Recommendations

### Recommendation 1 — Improve Product-Page → Cart Conversion

Investigate why users who view products do not add them to their cart.

Possible actions:
- Improve product-page clarity and value communication.
- Make pricing, shipping, and product information more visible.
- Strengthen calls to action.
- Reduce friction around product selection.

### Recommendation 2 — Investigate Acquisition Quality

Analyze high-volume traffic sources with relatively weak downstream conversion.

Possible actions:
- Compare landing pages by traffic source.
- Analyse search intent versus products viewed.
- Identify sources that generate high engagement but low purchase intent.
- Optimize campaigns toward users with stronger purchase intent.

### Recommendation 3 — Improve Retention

The cohort results show a large decline in activity after the initial month.

Possible actions:
- Introduce post-purchase engagement campaigns.
- Recommend relevant products based on previous activity.
- Use personalized offers or reminders.
- Test re-engagement messages for inactive users.

### Recommendation 4 — Focus on the Largest Funnel Drop-Off

Use funnel-stage conversion rates rather than raw user counts to prioritize experiments.

The first priority should be the stage where the largest proportion of users are lost, while continuing to monitor downstream stages.

---

## 3. Proven Findings vs Hypotheses

### Proven Findings

These are directly supported by the analysed dataset:

- Users drop substantially between product viewing and cart addition.
- Checkout users have a higher likelihood of completing a purchase than users at earlier funnel stages.
- Device-level conversion differences are relatively small.
- Major traffic sources contribute significantly different volumes of users.
- Cohort retention declines sharply after the initial month.

### Hypotheses

These are possible explanations that require additional investigation or experimentation:

- Users may abandon product pages because of unclear pricing or shipping information.
- Traffic sources with low conversion may have weaker purchase intent.
- Product-page design or calls to action may be creating friction.
- Lack of personalized follow-up may contribute to weak retention.
- Re-engagement campaigns may increase repeat activity.

> **Important:** Hypotheses should not be presented as confirmed causes unless supported by additional data or experiments.

## 5. Limitations

- The analysis is based on the available GA4 sample dataset and may not represent the behaviour of a real production e-commerce business.
- The dataset is observational, so correlations should not automatically be interpreted as causal relationships.
- Traffic source analysis identifies differences between sources but does not prove why those differences occur.
- Device-level differences are not sufficient by themselves to explain user behaviour.
- Revenue-related conclusions are limited by the available revenue fields and analysis performed.
- Retention analysis is based on observed user activity and does not establish the reasons users return or become inactive.
- A/B testing is required to establish whether proposed changes actually cause improvements.
- Some recommendations are hypotheses that require additional segmentation, qualitative research, or experimentation.

---

## Final Strategy Summary

### Primary Growth Opportunities

1. Improve the product-view → cart stage.
2. Investigate the quality and intent of high-volume acquisition sources.
3. Strengthen user retention and repeat engagement.
4. Validate proposed solutions through controlled A/B testing.

The final business strategy should clearly distinguish between:

- **What the data proves**
- **What the analysis suggests**
- **What still needs to be tested**
